Zapstick — presentation films, V5

Eight localized editions: French, English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish,
German, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Russian.
Duration: 35 seconds each. 26 varied shots. No voice-over.
Video: 1280 x 720, 30 fps, H.264 BT.709 limited-range yuv420p, AAC stereo.

Graphics, localized storyboard and motion design created for Zapstick.
Official Zapstick mark: /brand/zapstick-mark.svg.
Meme visuals reuse the locally curated files in /memes/.
Their source URLs and uploader credits are listed in /memes/SOURCES.txt.
The underlying meme visuals retain their owners' rights.

Typography uses Lilita One, Rubik, Noto Sans JP and Noto Sans SC from Google Fonts,
with renamed subsets and deterministic HarfBuzz-shaped SVG outlines.
Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lilitaone
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/rubik
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansjp
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
Complete SIL Open Font Licenses accompany the reproducible font subsets in
scripts/intro/fonts/ in the repository. Fonts are not fetched by visitors.

Original procedural music: 150 BPM, dry drums, syncopated bass, short chord hits,
and transition effects. No sampled music or generated voice is used.
