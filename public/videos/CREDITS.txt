Zapstick — presentation films, V2

Three localized editions: French, English and Brazilian Portuguese.
Duration: 120 seconds each. Original instrumental and sound design; no voice-over.
Video: 1280 x 720, 30 fps, H.264 BT.709 limited-range yuv420p, AAC stereo.

Graphics, localized storyboard and kinetic animation created for Zapstick.
Official Zapstick mark: /brand/zapstick-mark.svg.
Meme visuals reuse the locally curated files in /memes/.
Their source URLs and uploader credits are listed in /memes/SOURCES.txt.
The underlying meme visuals retain their owners' rights.

The soundtrack is an original procedural composition made for these films:
120 BPM, fifteen eight-second chapters, synthesized instruments and percussion,
with original whooshes, impacts, plops and typing aligned to the animation.
No sampled recording, downloaded music or synthetic voice is used.

The vector editor scenes illustrate Zapstick's workflow; they are not recordings
of a personal collection or a messaging account.

Reproducible source files are in scripts/intro/ in the Zapstick repository:
storyboard.json, music.py, render.mjs and create-captions.mjs.
